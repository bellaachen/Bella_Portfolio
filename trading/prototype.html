Drag & Drop Trading — Interaction Rules

1. Create an order
  • Right-click on chart (long-press on touch) → context menu shows Buy / Sell {units} @ cursor price, with order type pre-computed.
  • Hover price axis → ⊕ creates a Buy draft at that price.
  • Draft = solid line with Cancel / Buy·Sell / TP / SL chips and a drag handle near the price axis. The submit button reads Buy/Sell, and becomes Confirm once TP or SL is attached. Enter submits, Esc discards.
2. Order type is derived from price vs. market
Below marketAbove market
Buy (vs Ask)Buy LimitBuy Stop
Sell (vs Bid)Sell StopSell Limit
  • Type flips live while dragging or when market crosses a draft.
  • Click the type segment on a draft to switch Buy ⇄ Sell (TP/SL mirror around entry).
  • Click "Units" on a draft to edit quantity.
3. TP / SL
  • Press & drag the TP/SL chip out of the entry line; a click places it at a default distance.
  • Tag previews P&L in USD (XAUUSD: 1 unit = 1 oz, $1 move = $1 per unit).
  • Moving the entry line carries TP/SL with it (offsets kept).
  • Validation clamps the line: Buy TP > ref, SL < ref; Sell reversed. Ref = entry price for orders, current close price for open positions.
4. Modify
  • Pending order (solid line): drag → preview state with Cancel / Confirm; × cancels the order.
  • Open position (grey line): entry fixed; add / drag / remove TP·SL, then Confirm; × closes at market.
  • One Click Trading ON: context-menu orders and line edits are sent immediately, no confirm step (⊕ still opens a draft).
5. Execution simulation
  • Pending orders fill when the market crosses the price; positions close on TP/SL hit.
  • Use Sim price ▲▼ (±$1.50 / 15 pips) to force crossings. Feedback: Order Request → Placed / Filled / Modified / Closed toasts + Journal.
1. 建立訂單
  • 在圖表上按右鍵（觸控裝置長按）→ 選單顯示 買入 / 賣出 {數量} @ 游標價格，並自動判斷訂單類型。
  • 滑鼠移到價格軸 → 點 ⊕ 以該價格建立買入草稿單。
  • 草稿單為實線，帶有 取消 / 買入·賣出 / TP / SL 按鈕，價格軸旁有拖曳點。送出按鈕顯示「買入/賣出」，加上 TP 或 SL 後變成 確認。Enter 送出、Esc 放棄。
2. 訂單類型依「價格相對市價」判斷
低於市價高於市價
買入（對 Ask）限價買入停損買入
賣出（對 Bid）停損賣出限價賣出
  • 拖曳中或市價穿越草稿價格時，類型即時切換。
  • 點草稿上的類型欄位可切換買入 ⇄ 賣出（TP/SL 以進場價為軸對調）。
  • 點「數量」可修改單位數。
3. 止盈 TP / 止損 SL
  • 按住 TP/SL 小標籤，從進場線拖出；單點則放在預設距離。
  • 標籤即時預覽 USD 損益（XAUUSD：1 單位 = 1 盎司，價格每動 $1 = 每單位 $1）。
  • 移動進場線時，TP/SL 會保持距離一起移動。
  • 防呆：買單 TP 必須高於參考價、SL 低於參考價，賣單相反；拖過界會卡在邊界。掛單的參考價為進場價，持倉為目前平倉價。
4. 修改
  • 掛單（實線）：拖曳後進入預覽，顯示 取消 / 確認；× 取消訂單。
  • 持倉（灰線）：進場價固定，可新增／拖曳／移除 TP·SL 後按確認；× 以市價平倉。
  • 一鍵交易開啟：右鍵選單下單與拖線修改會直接送出，不需確認（⊕ 仍會開草稿）。
5. 成交模擬
  • 市價穿越掛單價時成交；持倉觸及 TP/SL 時平倉。
  • 用 模擬價格 ▲▼（±$1.50 / 15 pips）強制穿價。回饋：訂單請求 → 已下單／已成交／已修改／已平倉提示，並寫入日誌。
